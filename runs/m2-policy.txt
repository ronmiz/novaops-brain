To determine whether you can use your personal laptop for work, we need to refer to the company's IT and security policies. 

The current policy on the use of personal devices for work is documented in the internal IT policy manual [documents/IT_Policies/Personal_Device_Usage_Policy § 1.2]. According to this policy, employees are generally allowed to use personal laptops for work, provided they meet specific security requirements and are registered with the IT department [documents/IT_Policies/Personal_Device_Usage_Policy § 2.4].

You should consult the latest version of this policy or contact the IT helpdesk for any updates or additional guidelines that may apply to your specific role or department [documents/IT_Policies/Personal_Device_Usage_Policy § 3.1]. 

If there are no recent updates or specific restrictions noted, you can proceed by registering your device with IT and ensuring it complies with the outlined security protocols.
